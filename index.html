How to read the ledger

Financial security

Each bank’s December 2025 non-performing loan (NPL) ratio is compared with the NBC peer aggregate: 8.3% for commercial banks, 13.2% for specialized banks. Equity divided by assets gives a second view of the balance sheet. Neither measure predicts withdrawal access or depositor losses.

Integrity

Each profile takes its label from the strongest documented event: direct sanctions and licence withdrawals first, then other official actions, related-party records, and reported disputes. An allegation is not a finding.

Coverage

Based on the August 2026 commercial and specialized bank registers, plus five withdrawn licences. Owners are identified only to the level supported by the cited document. Dated edition with partial shareholder and sanctions coverage.
